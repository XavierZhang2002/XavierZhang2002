## Hi there, I'm Zhenliang Zhang 👋

I'm a master's student at [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn). My research focuses on **LLM post-training for real-world agentic scenarios**, including long-text understanding, financial analysis, and co-work tasks. My earlier work on LLM reliability progressed from causal analysis of hallucinations ([CIKM 2025](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3746252.3761298)), through hallucination detection ([ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.880/)), to representation-level intervention for controlled generation ([AAAI 2026](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40793)). I explored these topics with [Prof. Xiaojun Wan](https://wanxiaojun.github.io/).

Currently, I am an intern on the [**Ling Team**](https://www.ant-ling.com/en/) at Ant Group, working on foundation model post-training to improve Agent Instruction Following (IF) in finance agent and co-work scenarios.

Previously, I was a research intern at Tencent WeChat, where I developed **SCOUT** ([ICML 2026](https://xavierzhang2002.github.io/scout-page/)), an agentic paradigm for million-token long-text understanding through active information foraging.
