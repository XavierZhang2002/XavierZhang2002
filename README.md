## Hi there, I'm Zhenliang Zhang 👋

I'm a 2nd-year Master's student at [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn), advised by [Prof. Xiaojun Wan](https://wanxiaojun.github.io/). My research has evolved from investigating **model mitigation, reliability, and interpretability**—with publications on hallucination detection ([ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.880/)), social bias causality ([CIKM 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.07753)), and copyright protection ([AAAI 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07001))—towards building **Long-Context Agents** and **Agentic RL** systems, where I currently focus on active information foraging and post-training methods for million-token text understanding at **Tencent WXG** (WeChat).
