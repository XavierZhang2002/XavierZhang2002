## Hi there, I'm Zhenliang Zhang 👋

I'm a Master's student at [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn), advised by [Prof. Xiaojun Wan](https://wanxiaojun.github.io/). My research focuses on **LLM post-training for real-world agentic scenarios**, including long-text understanding and financial analysis. Previously, I studied LLM reliability through a progression from causal analysis of hallucinations ([CIKM 2025](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3746252.3761298)), through reliable hallucination detection ([ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.880/)), to representation-level intervention and controlled generation ([AAAI 2026](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/40793)).

Currently, I am an intern on the [**Ling Team**](https://www.ant-ling.com/en/) at **Ant Group**, working on Agent Instruction Following (IF) post-training. Previously, I was a research intern at **Tencent WeChat**, where I developed **SCOUT** ([ICML 2026](https://xavierzhang2002.github.io/scout-page/)), an agentic active information-foraging paradigm for million-token long-text understanding.
